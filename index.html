The host

AB

Professor Ashley Braganza

Chair in Business Transformation, Brunel University

Professor Braganza has held several leadership roles, including Dean of Brunel Business School, Deputy Dean of the College of Business, Arts and Social Sciences, and Head of Economics and Finance.

He founded and co-directs Brunel's interdisciplinary Research Centre for Artificial Intelligence, launched in 2018, and devised the university's Masters in AI Strategy, aimed at training the next generation of AI leaders.

The podcast brings an approachable, informative take on complex AI topics — for curious beginners, leaders running AI projects, and tech-savvy professionals alike.
